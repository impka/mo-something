1. What is the product’s name (your Mo-Something app)?
MoVids
2. What is the function + connection?
MoVids uses AI (I had something like Sora in mind) to generate videos 
for social media like YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, X, etc. 

3. Why did you choose to make the product?
With AI like Sora, engaging generated videos are much more viable now. 
I think having videos related to your small business is a good way to boost engagement
and market your product.

4. Why would MoFlo clients (small and medium sized businesses) be interested in and/or
use this product?
MoFlo clients would want to use this as it provides an easy way to create content
for their social media that is more engaging than say, a text post or image.

5. What would you improve or change about your new product if you had more time or
could work on it in the future?
I would do several things:
    1. Integrate it with OpenAI so it actually uses Sora
    2. Match it more closely with MoFlo's style
    3. Add a function where it automatically uploads videos on social media.
